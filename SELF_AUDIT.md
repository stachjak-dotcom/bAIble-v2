# bAIble v2 — Self-Audit

Before publishing or materially changing bAIble, check: purpose and scope are clear; a beginner can start without private context; principles are distinguishable from examples; facts, assumptions, hypotheses, decisions and unknowns can be distinguished; human authority remains visible; evidence and verification are distinct from self-report; public/private boundaries are clear; optional architecture is introduced only when a real need exists; referenced files exist; implementation claims have accessible evidence; generalized lessons do not expose private origins; sensitive identifiers and credentials are absent.

A failed item triggers investigation; it is not by itself proof that the underlying idea is wrong.
