# bAIble v2 — Self-Audit

Before publishing or materially changing bAIble, check all of the following:

- purpose and scope are clear;
- a beginner can start without private context;
- Human View and AI View remain understandable;
- principles are distinguishable from examples and culture;
- facts, observations, interpretations, hypotheses, decisions, evidence and unknowns are distinguishable;
- Reality Check, Fact Check, Verification and Acceptance are not collapsed into one mechanism;
- human authority remains visible;
- rAIda, Watchdog, Wolf and WheeAIls do not silently acquire authority;
- evidence and verification are distinct from self-report;
- contradictions expose the actual conflicting claims/sources, not just a yellow status;
- public/private boundaries are clear;
- provenance is preserved;
- relation links do not silently transfer truth, scope, authority or visibility;
- implementation claims have accessible evidence;
- referenced files exist;
- generalized lessons do not expose private origins;
- sensitive identifiers and credentials are absent;
- optional architecture exists only where real need justifies it;
- migration/history material does not override current navigation;
- runtime/test success is not presented as proof of editorial completeness.

A failed item triggers investigation. It is not by itself proof that the underlying idea is wrong.
