# bAIble v2 — Context & Memory

## Core rule

REMEMBER-TO-CHECK, NOT REMEMBER-EVERYTHING.

Durable memory exists to preserve enough context, provenance and recovery points to continue disciplined work. It is not a substitute for checking relevance, evidence or current meaning.

The detailed data contract for an addressable context record lives in `CONTEXT_ITEM.md`. This document defines how durable context should be treated.

## Context is not truth

Old context may be correct but irrelevant, stale, incomplete, superseded, contradictory, private or out of scope.

A stored record is not automatically true. A relationship is not proof. A remembered term is not proof that its original meaning survived.

Forbidden for material decisions:

`OLD MEMORY → NEW ASSUMPTION → ACTION`

Prefer:

`CONTEXT → CHECK RELEVANCE → GROUND → DECIDE → ACT`

## Context continuity is not meaning continuity

Keeping the same files, identifiers, terms or task history does not guarantee that a later worker interprets them in the same way.

When meaning matters, preserve or recover enough provenance and perspective to answer:

- what was actually received or observed,
- what was inferred,
- what the term or decision meant in its original context,
- what changed,
- what remains uncertain,
- what needs checking now.

Reality Check is the alignment mechanism when human and AI working maps may have diverged. Fact Check is the verification mechanism when a claim itself needs evidential checking.

## Missing context

If material context is missing, do not complete the story from plausibility.

`MISSING_CONTEXT → RECOVER / ASK / CHECK → CONTINUE`

Unknown, missing, stale or conflicting context should remain visible until resolved or deliberately bounded.

## Durable history

Do not rewrite old context merely to fit a newer interpretation.

Prefer adding a new observation, interpretation, decision or relationship that refines, contradicts or supersedes the earlier one while preserving provenance.

UnAiversed is a contextual mapping space, not a truth store.
