# bAIble v2 — Canonical Ecosystem Semantic Model

## Center

**bAIble + FederAItion + UnAiversed form one coordinated ecosystem.**

This is an architectural metaphor for three complementary domains, not a hierarchy and not a religious statement.

### Active entities

**HI — Human Intelligence**
- intent,
- values,
- material decisions,
- acceptance,
- intervention gates,
- responsibility for consequential choices.

**AI — Artificial Intelligence**
- bounded interpretation,
- planning,
- execution,
- checking,
- recording,
- assistance according to role, scope and evidence.

HI and AI jointly create, use, repair and evolve the ecosystem.

## Three primary domains

### bAIble
Answers mainly:
- how should we work,
- why does a guardrail exist,
- what vocabulary and roles are shared,
- how do lessons become reusable,
- what must remain explicit.

### FederAItion
Answers mainly:
- what task is active,
- who/what is authorized,
- what state is the work in,
- what was actually executed,
- what evidence exists,
- what transition may happen next.

### UnAiversed
Answers mainly:
- what is related to what,
- in which context,
- from which origin,
- with what status,
- where meanings, claims, decisions or evidence diverge.

UnAiversed is a map space, not a truth store.

## Graph rule

This document defines the canonical semantic model. `ECOSYSTEM_GRAPH.json` is its machine-readable seed; `ECOSYSTEM_MAP.md`, `HUMAN_VIEW.md`, `AI_VIEW.md` and future purpose-specific maps are views over the same model.

The graph is canonical; visualizations are views.

A relation should be explicit rather than inferred from proximity.

Suggested relation types:
- contains
- belongsTo
- coordinates
- invokes
- observes
- verifies
- producesEvidenceFor
- dependsOn
- protects
- constrains
- teaches
- remembersToCheck
- contradicts
- refines
- supersedes
- derivedFrom
- originatedFrom
- appliesTo
- handsOffTo
- monitors
- escalatesTo
- authorizes
- blocks
- exposesRiskTo
- learnsFrom
- represents
- linksContextTo
- requiresHumanDecision

A relation is not automatically true merely because it exists in the graph.

## Fundamental distinctions

FOUND ≠ UNDERSTOOD ≠ VERIFIED ≠ CANONICAL

IMPLEMENTED ≠ VERIFIED ≠ ACCEPTED

CONTEXT CONTINUITY ≠ MEANING CONTINUITY

RECORD ≠ TRUTH

DECISION ≠ TRUTH

RELATION ≠ EVIDENCE

AGREEMENT BETWEEN AGENTS ≠ INDEPENDENT VERIFICATION
