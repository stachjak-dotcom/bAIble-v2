# bAIble v2 — Ecosystem / Universe View

This document is a **view over the canonical semantic graph**, not a second source of truth.

Canonical semantic model: [SYSTEM_MAP.md](SYSTEM_MAP.md)  
Machine-readable seed: [ECOSYSTEM_GRAPH.json](ECOSYSTEM_GRAPH.json)

The purpose of this view is to show the ecosystem as a navigable relational universe: what exists, why it exists, what it relates to, what may trust or act on it, and what can distort it.

## Center

The ecosystem is formed by three distinct but gravitationally connected domains:

- **bAIble** — governance, language, rules, lessons, roles and reusable workflows.
- **FederAItion** — runtime, orchestration, execution state, evidence and operational continuity.
- **UnAiversed** — context, relationships, perspectives and semantic mapping.

They are not a hierarchy and none is the universal truth store.

**HI — Human Intelligence** and **AI — Artificial Intelligence** are participating entities that create, use, inspect, correct and evolve the ecosystem. They are not merely components inside it.

A compact conceptual center is:

`HI + AI ↔ bAIble + FederAItion + UnAiversed`

## Universe geometry

Do not render this as a simple mind-map tree.

Treat the three primary domains as overlapping / gravitationally bound galaxies. An object may belong to several contexts at once, and a relation may be as important as the connected nodes.

The view should make it possible to zoom from philosophy to operational detail without changing the underlying semantic identity.

## Level-4 reading

### L1 — Constellations
bAIble / FederAItion / UnAiversed / HI / AI

At distance, this answers:
- what the ecosystem is,
- who participates,
- which domain is responsible for what.

### L2 — Systems
rAIda / Watchdog / Reality Check / Fact Check / Verification / roles / memory / learning / onboarding / context / security / evidence.

At this level, the viewer should reveal the major systems and responsibility boundaries.

### L3 — Mechanisms
preflight / scope checks / evidence chains / handoffs / Context Items / task lifecycle / authorization / public-private boundaries / evals / recovery / persistence / continuity.

At this level, the viewer should reveal how work actually moves.

### L4 — Atomic objects and relations
observation / claim / interpretation / decision / rule / lesson / evidence / verification / question / experiment / risk / source / authority / state / relation / limitation / next check / contradiction.

At maximum detail, the viewer should be able to explain why one concrete mechanism may or may not change another state.

## Relations are first-class objects

Do not render anonymous lines when the relationship itself matters.

Representative relation types include:

`contains`, `belongsTo`, `coordinates`, `invokes`, `observes`, `verifies`, `producesEvidenceFor`, `dependsOn`, `protects`, `constrains`, `teaches`, `remembersToCheck`, `contradicts`, `refines`, `supersedes`, `derivedFrom`, `originatedFrom`, `appliesTo`, `handsOffTo`, `monitors`, `escalatesTo`, `authorizes`, `blocks`, `exposesRiskTo`, `learnsFrom`, `represents`, `linksContextTo`, `requiresHumanDecision`.

A material relation may itself carry:
- provenance,
- context/scope,
- evidence,
- status,
- confidence where useful,
- temporal validity,
- verification state,
- limitations,
- next check.

`RELATION ≠ EVIDENCE`

## “Požírači hvězd” — local failure anomalies

Failure modes are not a detached risk appendix. They should appear close to the objects/transitions they can deform.

Examples:
- **Context Drift** — AI ↔ UnAiversed ↔ task context.
- **Meaning Drift** — language / named mechanisms / persisted context.
- **Scope Drift** — human intent ↔ rAIda ↔ agent execution.
- **False Done** — execution ↔ evidence ↔ verification.
- **False Continuity** — handoff / recovered state ↔ receiving worker.
- **Activity Substitution** — intent ↔ next authorized action ↔ execution.
- **Memory Inflation** — context activation / UnAiversed.
- **Automation Without Authority** — rAIda / Watchdog / task transitions.
- **Record ≠ Truth** — Repository / Context Items / UnAiversed.
- **Decision ≠ Truth** — human/system decisions over time.
- **User Adaptation Hides Failure** — Human ↔ AI collaboration loop.

A spatial renderer may represent them differently from normal nodes — for example as local anomalies or gravitational distortions — because they are not normal system components.

## Different views, same universe

The canonical graph is shared, but views may have different navigation logic.

### Human View
Optimizes for questions such as:
- What is this?
- Why does it exist?
- What are we trying to achieve?
- What is happening now?
- What needs my decision?
- What is known, uncertain or contradictory?
- Who may act?
- Where is the problem?

### AI View
Optimizes for:
- What is the intent?
- What is my role?
- What is my scope?
- Which sources are authorized?
- What is verified?
- What is only observed / claimed / interpreted?
- What may I modify?
- What evidence is expected?
- What requires human approval?
- What is the next authorized transition?

## History

History should appear only when it explains current structure or protects meaning.

Useful example:
the separation of rAIda, Watchdog, Reality Check and Fact Check explains present responsibility boundaries.

Routine commit chronology does not belong in the main universe.

## Visual epistemics

Not every object on the map is equally “real” in the epistemic sense.

A viewer should be able to distinguish at least:

OBSERVATION / CLAIM / INTERPRETATION / DECISION / RULE / LESSON / EVIDENCE / VERIFICATION / MECHANISM / ROLE / SYSTEM / RISK / QUESTION / EXPERIMENT.

Storage, repetition or visual prominence must never silently promote an item to truth.

## Design principle

**The map must not merely show what exists. It should show why it exists, what it relates to, what may trust it, who may act on it, and what happens when it fails.**

That is the difference between a decorative mind map and UnAiversed as a semantic/spatial representation.
